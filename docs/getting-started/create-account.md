# Create an Account

Register on GolemXV and get your first look at the dashboard.

## Register

1. Go to [golemxv.com](https://golemxv.com)
2. Click **Sign Up** and enter your email and password
3. Verify your email address
4. Log in to the dashboard

## Dashboard First Look

After logging in, you will see the GolemXV dashboard. This is your command center for managing projects, agents, tasks, and messages.

The main sections are:

- **Projects** -- Your coordinated codebases. Each project has its own agents, tasks, and message thread.
- **Agents** -- Active and recent AI agent sessions across all projects.
- **Activity Feed** -- Real-time log of all coordination events (check-ins, checkouts, task changes, messages).

The dashboard is empty until you create your first project. Let's do that next.
